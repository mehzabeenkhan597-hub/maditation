About the Coach

A Decade of Mindful Living

"My mission is to guide you home — to the stillness that was always within you."

With over a decade of dedicated meditation practice and professional certification from internationally recognized mindfulness institutions, I have helped hundreds of students transform their inner world — and, in turn, their outer lives.

My journey began after experiencing burnout in my early career. Through meditation, breathwork, and spiritual exploration across India, Bali, and Thailand, I discovered a path back to peace — and made it my life's purpose to share it.

I hold certifications in MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction), Vipassana, Kundalini Yoga, and Chakra Healing. Every session is rooted in authentic tradition and adapted for modern life.

FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

What exactly is meditation? +
Meditation is the practice of training the mind to focus and redirect thoughts. It cultivates awareness, presence, and a deep sense of inner peace. Far from emptying the mind, it teaches you to observe your thoughts without being controlled by them.
How can complete beginners start? +
Perfectly — all sessions are designed to welcome you exactly where you are. We begin with simple breath awareness, building gradually. Many of my most transformed students came to me having never meditated a single day in their lives.
Are online sessions as effective as in-person? +
Absolutely. I've worked with students across three continents via video call. The inner work happens within you — presence and connection travel across screens beautifully. Many clients prefer the comfort of meditating in their own sacred space.
How long is a typical session? +
Individual sessions are typically 60 minutes. Group meditation sessions run for 45–90 minutes. Intensive workshops span a full day (6–7 hours). I also offer 30-minute check-in sessions for returning students.
Is meditation effective for anxiety? +
Yes — extensively so. Dozens of peer-reviewed studies confirm meditation's effectiveness in reducing anxiety. The practices I use are specifically adapted from MBSR, which was developed precisely to address chronic stress and anxiety disorders.
Do you offer personal 1-on-1 coaching? +
Yes. Personal coaching is actually my most popular offering. These sessions are completely bespoke — built around your specific life, challenges, and aspirations. We move at your pace, with your transformation as the only agenda.
